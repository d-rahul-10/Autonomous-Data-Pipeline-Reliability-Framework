PIPELINEIQ POLICIES

Privacy Policy

PipelineIQ uses the account name and email address you provide to create and manage your sign-in. Passwords are stored as secure hashes and are not saved as readable text.

Pipeline logs and CSV files you submit are processed by the application to validate data, analyze pipeline reliability, and create reports. Application data and generated reports are stored in the app’s configured local storage. Avoid uploading passwords, access tokens, or other secrets in pipeline files.

The app uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. You can end that session with the Log out control. Local data may remain in the configured storage after you log out; contact support if you need help removing it.
